End-to-End Cryptographic & AI Pipeline
How incident reports progress from field observation to immutable on-chain consensus
STAGE 01
Field Ingestion
Volunteers capture incident description, photo evidence, and auto-detect GPS coordinates within their assigned municipal ward.
STAGE 02
Multi-Modal Fusion
2D CNN processes text tokens while Gemini Vision parses imagery to verify disaster cues and compute the 0–100 Explainable Trust Score.
STAGE 03
Blockchain Anchoring
A canonical SHA-256 digest is generated and recorded on Ethereum smart contract (`Event.sol`) with transaction receipts logged in Supabase.
STAGE 04
EOC Dispatch
Disaster Management Officers review the live tactical GEOINT map, inspect cryptographic proof, and initiate emergency first-responder deployment.
